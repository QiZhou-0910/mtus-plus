About This List

The codes and labels come from the MTUS+ Codebook. Each classification is available for up to four activity layers (pri_, sec_, ter_, quat_). ICATUS and HETUS come at three levels of detail.

  • ICATUS 2016 is detailed for work and production; it suits cross-national analysis and surveys from low- and middle-income countries.
  • HETUS 2018 is detailed for leisure and personal activities; it suits analysis focused on high-income countries.
  • CTUR 69 continues the legacy MTUS activity scheme.

In the HETUS lists, rows in capitals are group-level categories. Not every code can be identified in every survey: check the coverage indicators (svyicat1–3, svyhetus1–3, svyctur) before you compare surveys.

Unobserved activity is system missing in ICATUS and HETUS. In CTUR 69 it is code 69, "no recorded activity".

To be confirmed: the codes follow the working draft of the Codebook; check them against the final Codebook before release
