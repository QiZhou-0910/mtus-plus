2d:T408,<p>MTUS+ stores up to four activities per episode: primary, secondary, tertiary
and quaternary. Primary activities add up to 1,440 minutes a day.
Simultaneous activities do not add to this total automatically. Activities
such as childcare are often done alongside something else, so
primary-activity totals can understate them.</p>
<p>How many layers a diary allows depends on its design (see <code>SVYNSIMS</code>).
Wide-interval diaries, common in LMIC surveys, allow several activities per
30- or 60-minute slot. More reported simultaneity can therefore reflect the
instrument rather than behaviour.</p>
<p>Choose the time-allocation rule that fits your question. The <code>timealloc</code>
command supports the common rules:</p>
