Questions

Short answers to the questions people ask most.

What Is MTUS+?

The redesigned Multinational Time Use Study. It harmonises national time-use diary surveys into one integrated, episode-level file. It keeps far more of each original survey than earlier releases while still providing common variables for comparison. See About MTUS+.

How Is It Different From MTUS?

Legacy MTUS keeps the familiar HAF and HEF files and the 69- and 25-activity schemes. MTUS+ has one Integrated File, three activity classifications (ICATUS 2016, HETUS 2018 and CTUR 69), up to four simultaneous activities, the original diary fields and roster-based household variables. Both continue.

Who Can Use It, And What Does It Cost?

It is free for non-commercial research. See Access for how to get the files.

Why Is There No File Of Daily Totals?

With several classifications and levels of detail there is no single natural set of totals, and detailed totals can hide gaps in a survey's own codes. Build the totals you need from the Integrated File with the timealloc command; see Using the data.

Which Weight Should I Use?

propwt for most analyses: it balances days of the week and gives low-quality diaries a weight of zero. Use propwt_all if you want to keep every diary and judge quality yourself. ocombwt is the original survey weight.

Can I Pool Countries Into One Sample?

Pooling mixes sample sizes, weight scales and sampling designs. Estimate within each survey first, then compare or combine the results with weights you choose deliberately, such as equal weight per country.

Why Do Surveys Have Different Numbers Of Episodes?

In fully harmonised surveys, episodes follow the original diary, so a survey with a detailed activity list has more, shorter episodes. Redefine episodes with epigenx before comparing episode counts or durations.

Why Does Minute 0 Mean Different Times?

Diary days start at different clock times in different surveys. svystart gives the start time; align to clock time before comparing timing across surveys.

Why Do Only Some Surveys Have Third And Fourth Activities?

Only some diaries allow more than two activities at once, usually those with 30- or 60-minute slots. svynsims gives the number of activity layers each survey allows. An empty layer is not evidence that nothing else was happening.

Which Activity Classification Should I Use?

ICATUS for work and production and for low- and middle-income countries; HETUS for leisure and personal activities in high-income countries; CTUR 69 for continuity with legacy MTUS. Check the coverage indicators first. See Activity codes.

Why Are Some Variables Stored As Text?

Non-harmonised variables, such as original education or religion, keep each survey's own categories. As text, those categories stay readable when surveys are combined.

Can I Get The Original Survey Data?

The Surveys page links to each producer's data and documentation. Access conditions are set by the producer.

Is AI Used In Harmonisation?

As an assistant only: to help read documentation, find variables and draft code. A harmoniser checks every suggestion against the original data, and AI never creates or imputes observations. See Harmonisation.

How Do I Cite MTUS+?

Cite the release you used. Where your work relies on how a particular survey was harmonised, cite its harmonisation syntax as well. See How to cite.

I Think I Found An Error. What Should I Do?

Email ctur@ucl.ac.uk with the survey, the variables and what you see. Corrections are announced on Notices.
