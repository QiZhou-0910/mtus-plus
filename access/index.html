Access

Free for non-commercial research.

Access Conditions

The harmonised data are made available free of charge to registered users, for research purposes only. Commercial use is not permitted. These are the terms CTUR agrees with the data producers when it asks permission to redistribute their surveys in harmonised form.

To be confirmed: the registration process, licence or end-user agreement wording, and whether users must accept terms before downloading

Where To Get The Data

Product Access route
MTUS+ Distributed directly by the Centre for Time Use Research through the UCL Research Data Repository and this website. To be confirmed: repository record URL and DOI, and what is available on each of the two sites
Legacy MTUS IPUMS MTUS online extract builder, which lets you browse metadata, choose surveys and variables, and build custom extracts. The current legacy release, MTUS Release 11, is also on the UCL Research Data Repository.

The longer-term aim is for both MTUS and MTUS+ to be available from CTUR and from IPUMS. To be confirmed: wording on future IPUMS distribution of MTUS+

Versions

MTUS and MTUS+ are versioned research resources. Each release stays identifiable and citable, so users can state exactly which version they used. MTUS+ has its own version sequence, starting at Version 1. To be confirmed: how earlier releases will be kept and accessed in the UCL repository and on this website

Surveys With Restricted Access

Some time-use microdata cannot be redistributed, even in harmonised form. This applies particularly to some Asian surveys, where access may require institutional partnerships or secure or remote environments. For these surveys, MTUS is developing arrangements under which the data are harmonised to MTUS conventions while national access rules are respected.

China Time Use Surveys (2017, 2021, 2025). These surveys are not distributed as part of MTUS+. They may be available through MTUS-related infrastructure under controlled or remote access. Users should cite both the original survey and the MTUS harmonisation syntax (see How to cite). To be confirmed: how researchers apply for access to the China Time Use Surveys

Surveys still awaiting redistribution permission will be added in later updates once permission is granted.
