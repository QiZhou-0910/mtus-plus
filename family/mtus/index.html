MTUS Family

MTUS

Where it all began.

Available

The original Multinational Time Use Study, created by Jonathan Gershuny and Sally Jones in the early 1980s, is the foundation on which the entire MTUS family has been built. This pioneering collection of harmonised time-use data remains available in its original legacy formats, preserving the project’s history and providing continuity for researchers working with established MTUS datasets.

The MTUS family

MTUS+ is produced by the Centre for Time Use Research, University College London. Free for non-commercial research. Day profiles on this site are weighted aggregates from the legacy MTUS episode files; no individual diary records are published.
