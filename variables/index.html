About This List

Names and labels come from the MTUS+ Codebook, which also gives each variable's coding, construction and comparability notes.

  • Key marks the variables most analyses need: identifiers, weights, diary day, basic demographics and the primary-activity variables.
  • Text, not harmonised marks variables kept with their original survey categories as text, so their meaning differs between surveys.

Combine country (or iso), survey, svyregion, hldid, persid, id and epnum to identify an episode. Drop the later identifiers to identify a diary, a person, a household or a survey.

To be confirmed: episode timing variables (start, end and duration) are used in the harmonisation template but are not yet documented in the Codebook
