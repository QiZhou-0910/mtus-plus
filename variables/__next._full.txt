1b:T486,<p>Names and labels come from the MTUS+ Codebook, which also gives each
variable&#39;s coding, construction and comparability notes.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Key</strong> marks the variables most analyses need: identifiers, weights,
diary day, basic demographics and the primary-activity variables.</li>
<li><strong>Text, not harmonised</strong> marks variables kept with their original survey
categories as text, so their meaning differs between surveys.</li>
</ul>
<p>Combine <code>country</code> (or <code>iso</code>), <code>survey</code>, <code>svyregion</code>, <code>hldid</code>, <code>persid</code>, <code>id</code>
and <code>epnum</code> to identify an episode. Drop the later identifiers to identify a
diary, a person, a household or a survey.</p>
